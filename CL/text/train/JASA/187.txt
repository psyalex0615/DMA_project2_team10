If the class of functions is sufficiently rich, then we are guaranteed to uncover the Fréchet SDR space. We showed that such a class, which we call an ensemble, can be generated by a universal kernel (cc-universal kernel). We established the consistency and asymptotic convergence rate of the proposed methods. The finite-sample performance of the proposed methods is illustrated through simulation studies for several commonly encountered metric spaces that include Wasserstein space, the space of symmetric positive definite matrices, and the sphere. We illustrated the data visualization aspect of our method by the human mortality distribution data from the United Nations Databases.
