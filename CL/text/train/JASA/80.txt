Our analysis uses a novel leave-one-out construction for the iterates, and our bounds depend only on spectral properties of the underlying low-rank tensor under nearly optimal signal-to-noise ratio conditions such that tensor SVD is computationally feasible. Finally, we apply our methodology to real and simulated data, demonstrating some effects not identifiable from the model with discrete community memberships.
