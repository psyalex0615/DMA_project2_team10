Discussion of ‘Estimating time-varying causal excursion effects in mobile health with binary outcomes’. This is an interesting paper on an important problem. As internet-enabled devices become increasingly ubiquitous, manufacturers and developers are employing randomized experiments to optimize the performance of their products. The methods presented in Qian et al. (2021) have close relationships to others in the literature, in particular to those in a series of papers by Robins, Hernán and collaborators on analysing observational studies as a series of randomized trials (Hernán et al., 2005, 2008; Hernán & Robins, 2017), also described as emulating a desired target randomized trial (Hernán & Robins, 2016). There is also a close relationship to the history-restricted marginal structural models of Neugebauer et al. (2007), and the history-adjusted marginal structural models of Joffe et al.
