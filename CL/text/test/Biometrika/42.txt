Correction to: ‘Simplified integrated nested Laplace approximation’. Theorem 1 of this paper is incorrect.The problem is a misapplication of Theorem 6.3 of Nocedal & Wright (2006).The property that holds is that H1 = H2 + O(h), where H2 is a matrix such that the eigenvalues of H1/2 H-1 2 H1/2 are bounded between the eigenvalues of H-1/2 H-1 0 H1/2 and 1.This is still a desirable property if approximating | H| by | H1 |, but it is much weaker than Theorem 1.I thank How Si Yu for pointing out the problem.
